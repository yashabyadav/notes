## Quotes / Figures

> We have demonstrated that compressive forces increase the density of growing filament ends at the leading edge of a branched actin network by decreasing the (per-filament) rate of capping, and not by increasing the overall rate of nucleation.

> The binding of capping protein to the free barbed end of an actin filament is a bi-molecular interaction, whose rate depends on the concentration of capping protein and the rate constant for filament capping, that is $k_{cap} [CP]$. All our results are consistent with the idea that force decreases the capping rate constant, $k_{cap}$, without changing the local concentration of capping protein.
