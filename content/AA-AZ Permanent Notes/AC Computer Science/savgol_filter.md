### Used for
Smoothening a graph.

Applies a Savitzky–Golay filter.

A Savitzky-Golay filter works by fitting successive sub-sets of adjacent data points with a low-degree polynomial by the method of linear least squares

Used this for smoothening nucleation rate plots.![[100xSmoothedPlot.png]]
